# Research in Fieldwork

Fieldwork publishes the reasoning behind its mechanisms, not private source material.

This directory will contain:

- an annotated bibliography linking to public papers and primary sources;
- sanitized descriptions of experiments and measurement methods;
- synthetic or aggregate fixtures sufficient to reproduce relevant findings;
- negative results and known limitations;
- the design decisions that followed from the evidence.

Papers are linked and cited rather than bundled unless redistribution rights are explicit. Private
transcripts, profiles, account data, local paths, personal assets, and identifying corpora are never
used as public fixtures. A finding derived from private material must be reproducible with a synthetic
or separately licensed specimen before it becomes a public test.
