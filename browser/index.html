            Task heartbeats

            Check on one task regularly

            A heartbeat has your agent check on one task on a schedule, using only the tools you tick. Each check ends as no change, changed, or finished. Checks with no change stay quiet, so you only hear real news. The first check comes one full interval after you create it.

            Tools it may use

            Only the tools you tick are offered, plus one for reporting the result. If you link a goal's measure, its tools are locked to that goal and measure, so the agent can't point them anywhere else.

            Goals & promises

            What your agent is working toward

            A goal says what success looks like and when to look again. A promise also says who it was made to and when it is due. Everyday reminders and maybes stay in Memory: nothing here becomes an obligation unless someone writes it down here.

            Measures (optional)

            Name where each number comes from before collecting it. Each check can record a value, say nothing changed, or say there was no data to read.

            New goal or promise

            To check a measure automatically, link a Task heartbeat to it. Each check still reports its result, and “no change” is a fine answer.

            Goals, promises, and their history

            Outside reader

            A second opinion, now and then

            On a schedule, a fresh copy of the model that doesn't know your agent reads the goals and promises you pick, and points out anything that looks stuck, contradictory, or unsupported. It can't change anything.

            What it can and can't see
            • It reads only the goals and promises you pick. New ones are not added on their own.
            • It runs on the current model without your agent's files, the chat, or the room's tools, and it can't edit what it reads.
            • What it points out are leads to check, not proof. If something is too long to read whole, the review stops instead of reading only part of it.
